Project Idea
Develop a text-based psychological horror game centered around Matt, a war veteran struggling with survivor guilt, trauma, and the collapse of his personal life. The player explores distorted locations and memories that reflect Matt’s mental state rather than a traditional physical world.

The story focuses on avoidance, memory reconstruction, guilt, and eventually being forced to confront the parts of himself he has tried to bury. Instead of using traditional NPCs, the enemies and major encounters represent damaged or twisted versions of Matt himself. As the player progresses, they uncover pieces of his past and slowly learn what happened, including the traumatic event that caused his mental state to fracture.

Goal is to create horror experience that relies on atmosphere, storytelling, choices and tension the best I can in a text based game

Project Standup Post
Progress Update 
This week I would like to work on laying the foundation for my text-based horror game. The goal is to developing the overall game concept, figuring out what systems I want the player to interact with, and breaking those ideas down into something I can realistically build in C++. One of the main systems I want to focus on is sanity. I do not want sanity to just be another number next to health. I want it to eventually affect how the player experiences the story, such as what they see, what information they can trust, and possibly what choices or outcomes become available. This is something I want to continue developing this game throughout my time at Full Sail as well. The biggest challenge so far has been controlling the scope of my ideas. Storytelling and atmosphere are the parts I naturally want to keep expanding, but I have to constantly bring myself back to the question of, “What gameplay system does this idea actually become, and how am I going to represent that in C++?” Breaking the larger horror concept into smaller pieces has probably been the most important part of Week 1. Since I'm not a great coder and more of storyteller this will be the hardest for me I think.
